Privacy

SmartRoute has no accounts. Your settings (home address, bridge choices) are saved only in this phone's browser.

To find addresses and routes, the addresses you type are sent to free public services: the US Census geocoder (through this site), Photon, Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) and the OSRM routing server. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. When you tap a Google Maps or Waze button, that app gets the trip.

StepFree reports you send are stored on this site and shown on the map (without your email). The email is optional and is only used to ask about your report.

Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter (no cookies).
